Ski Apex 0.5.0
- How was the snow? At the end of a day you say (powder, groomed, icy...) and who opens the ski area sees it, with the average day of who skied there.
- Go on on the Watch: start on the iPhone, pass the day to the Watch and put the phone away. At the end of the day it all arrives on the iPhone.
- The page of a ski area: pistes, lifts, altitudes, today's snow hour by hour and the snow of the last week.
- Downloaded maps for when there is no signal, and the name of the piste while you ski.
- Season recap to share (from Stats).
