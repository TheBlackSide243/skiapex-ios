First version of Ski Apex, the diary of your days on skis.
- Tap Start at the bottom of the slope and put your iPhone in your pocket: it records with the screen off too.
- It recognizes runs and lifts on its own, with top speed, vertical, distance and altitude.
- In Days you find the map of each day, the altitude chart and the details of every run.
- No snow? In Settings you can make a sample day.
