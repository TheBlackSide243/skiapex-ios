Ski Apex 0.4.0
- Apple Watch: records the day by itself with its GPS, and sends it to the iPhone when you finish. Or works as a remote when the iPhone records.
- Piste map: the pistes, lifts and huts of a ski area, and the way from one point to another (from the Guide).
- Profile pictures, and Sign in with Apple.
- New Settings, with looks and app icons (Settings, Look).
- Widgets: the snow to come at a place of your choice, and a button to record.
- Friends live on the map while you record, with a notice when one is close; over Bluetooth even with no signal.
To test: a recording started from the Watch, until it shows up in Days; friends live with two people.
