Ski Apex 0.4.0
- Piste map: the pistes, lifts and huts of a ski area, and the way from one point to another (from the Guide).
- Apple Watch: the numbers of the day on your wrist and the buttons to start, pause and finish.
- Widgets: the snow to come at a place of your choice, and a button to record.
- Looks and icons: six looks for the app and the icon in the same colors (Settings, Look).
- Friends live on the map while you record, with a notice when one is close; over Bluetooth even with no signal (Friends tab).
