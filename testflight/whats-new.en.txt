Ski Apex 0.2.0
- Piste and lift names (from OpenStreetMap) with the color of the difficulty, and the weather of the day.
- Timeline of the day: runs, lifts and breaks in order.
- Replay the day on the map with time, speed and altitude.
- Every run drawn on the map in the colors of its speed.
- New Stats tab: totals, averages, records and charts.
- Picture to share with the track and the numbers.
- Import GPX files from other apps.
- Ski, snowboard, telemark or sled for each day.
