Ski Apex 0.8.0
- Your pistes: for each piste how many times you skied it, your best time and your top speed. In Days, Statistics, Your pistes; and on the page of a ski area and of a run.
- Challenge on a piste: turn it on in Friends and each piste shows the ranking of the times with your friends.
- Meeting point: in Explore touch a hut, a piste or a lift and choose Let's meet here. Your friends see it on the map, with the way to get there.
- New pictures to share, as a post or as a story.
- The Watch and the widgets in the look of the app, and a complication to open Ski Apex from the watch face.
- A copy of the days in iCloud: the switch is in Settings, Backup, but in this build iCloud is not on yet.
To test: open Your pistes and one piste; turn the challenge on with a friend; send a meeting point from Explore; share a day in both formats; the complication and the new screens on the Watch.
- The profile of the day: up with the lifts, down the pistes in the color of their signs. On every day of the list and at the top of its page.
- Piste badges, with color and number as on the signs; on the map the runs take the color of their piste, and the places you skied are marked with the lens of the icon.
- The ski area on the map: open a ski area from the Guide and the map behind shows it with all its pistes and lifts.
- A new icon: ski goggles with the peak in their lens. In Settings, Look, choose among four drawings, and the first in the other colors too.
- On the iPhones with the clock and the battery on the right edge of the screen the buttons go in that band: no more empty space on the right.
- At the bottom of every screen there is room to scroll clear of the round button.
- Skiing, snowboarding, telemark and sled are all in sight, with no scrolling.
- A new look: the map is always behind, and the screens are on a sheet you pull up and down. On the right your picture (Settings), Days, Guide, Friends and the round button that records.
- Winter map: snow, shade and contour lines, with the mountains at night from far away.
- What are you riding today? Skiing, snowboarding, telemark or sled: you choose before you start.
